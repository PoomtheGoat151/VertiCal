Phase 1: Foundation. Do it 3 days a week with a rest day between sessions. Weeks 1 to 4.

Warm up 8 min. Rest 90 sec between sets.
Bodyweight squats3 × 12
Box step-ups3 × 8 each leg
Pogo hops3 × 20 sec
Stick-the-landing drops3 × 6
Calf raises3 × 15

Day 2: Jump basics

Warm up 8 min. Rest 2 min between sets.
Squat jumps4 × 6
Countermovement jumps4 × 5
Broad jumps3 × 5

Plank3 × 30 sec

Day 3: Power and balance

Warm up 8 min. Rest 90 sec between sets.
Box jumps (low box)4 × 5
Lateral bounds3 × 8 each side
Single-leg hops in place3 × 8 each leg
Reverse lunges3 × 10 each leg
Max-effort vertical jumps5 × 3

Stop if you feel sharp pain. Land softly with bent knees. Check with a doctor before starting if you have injuries.
